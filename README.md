# Legal Doc Generator

A Python tool that generates a Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions for small websites and businesses, based on a short set of questions.

## What it does

Answer a few basic questions (business name, website, country, whether you collect emails, whether you use cookies), and it generates:
- A ready-to-use Privacy Policy
- A ready-to-use Terms & Conditions document

## Why it's useful

Every website legally needs a Privacy Policy and Terms page, but most small business owners don't have one or don't know how to write one. This automates that in minutes instead of hiring a lawyer for a standard document.

## Tech Stack

Python

## How to use

1. Run: `python generator.py`
2. Answer the questions it asks
3. Check `privacy_policy.txt` and `terms_and_conditions.txt` for your generated documents
